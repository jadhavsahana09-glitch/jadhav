📚 Learn & Act

Daily eco challenges & sustainability tips

Stay engaged with rotating daily challenges, carbon facts, and climate news.

🌍

Did you know?

The average Indian emits ~1.9 tonnes of CO₂ per year — the global average is 4.7 tonnes. Sustainable choices can halve that.

🥩

Food Footprint Fact

Producing 1 kg of beef emits ~27 kg of CO₂. Switching to chicken cuts that 4×, while lentils cut it 50× further.

🚌

Transport Tip

Taking a bus instead of a car for your daily commute can save up to 2.4 kg CO₂ per day — that's 720 kg per year!

💡

Energy Win

Replacing 5 incandescent bulbs with LEDs saves ~300 kWh/year — equivalent to planting 2 trees annually.

🛒

Shopping Smart

Fast fashion produces 10% of global CO₂. Buying second-hand or choosing quality over quantity makes a measurable difference.

🔒 Security & Privacy

Your data is safe with us

Enterprise-grade security is built into every layer of Carby Control — because trust is the foundation of a sustainable platform.

verified_user

CSRF Protection

Every form submission is protected with server-side CSRF tokens. Cross-site request forgery attacks are blocked automatically.

✅ Active
lock

OTP-Based Auth

No passwords stored. Login via time-limited OTP sent to email or SMS. Even if intercepted, codes expire in 10 minutes.

✅ Active
security

Session Encryption

Flask sessions are signed with a 256-bit secret key using HMAC-SHA1. Session data cannot be tampered with by clients.

✅ Active
speed

OTP Rate Limiting

OTP tokens are invalidated after use and expire automatically. Old tokens are purged on each new request to prevent replay attacks.

✅ Active
storage

Parameterized Queries

All database interactions use parameterized queries across MySQL, PostgreSQL, and SQLite — SQL injection is structurally impossible.

✅ Active
https

HTTPS Enforcement

All data in transit is encrypted via TLS. Deployed on Vercel with automatic SSL certificate management.
